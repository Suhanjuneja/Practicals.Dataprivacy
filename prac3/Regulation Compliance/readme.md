## Title

**Regulation Compliance Plan for Porter (porter.in)**

## 1. Introduction

Porter is an Indian logistics platform that connects customers with Driver Partners for goods transportation. During its operations, the platform collects personal information such as names, phone numbers, pickup and delivery addresses, location details and payment-related information.

Since this information belongs to customers and drivers, Porter must follow appropriate data protection regulations. This practical examines the company's privacy practices and develops a compliance plan based on Indian data protection laws.

## 2. Applicable Regulations

The following regulations are considered:

### A. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

This Act provides a legal framework for protecting digital personal information in India.

Important requirements include:

- **Consent and lawful processing:** Personal data must be processed on a valid legal basis.
- **Purpose limitation:** Information should only be used for specified lawful purposes.
- **Data minimisation:** Only necessary personal information should be collected.
- **Transparency:** Users should be informed about data collection and usage.
- **Data security:** Organisations must implement reasonable security safeguards.
- **Data deletion:** Information should be erased when no longer required, subject to legal exceptions.
- **User rights:** Individuals should have access to applicable rights such as correction, erasure and grievance redressal.

### B. Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025

These Rules explain how organisations should implement the DPDP Act.

Important requirements include:

- Providing clear privacy notices.
- Maintaining appropriate security safeguards.
- Establishing procedures for personal data breach notifications.
- Providing mechanisms for users to exercise their rights.
- Following applicable data retention and deletion requirements.

### C. Information Technology Act, 2000 and SPDI Rules, 2011

These provisions address electronic information security and the handling of sensitive personal information.

They are relevant when examining Porter's handling of financial information and other protected personal details.

**Note:** The DPDP framework has phased implementation dates. Its main substantive obligations are scheduled to commence in May 2027.

## 4. Key Compliance Requirements

### 4.1 Purpose Limitation

Customer and driver information should only be used for purposes such as booking management, transportation, payment processing and safety.

### 4.2 Data Minimisation

Porter should collect only the information necessary to provide its services.

### 4.3 Transparency

Users should clearly understand what information is collected, why it is collected and with whom it may be shared.

### 4.4 Consent

Where consent is the applicable legal basis, users should receive clear information and appropriate control over their personal data.

### 4.5 Data Security

The company should implement encryption, authentication, restricted access and regular security assessments.

### 4.6 Data Retention

Customer addresses, location records and trip information should not be retained longer than necessary, except where legally required.

### 4.7 User Rights

Porter should provide accessible mechanisms for users to request correction, deletion and other applicable data protection rights.

### 4.8 Third-Party Sharing

Information shared with payment processors and service providers should be protected through appropriate safeguards and agreements.

## 6. Compliance Assessment of Porter

Based on my examination of Porter's published Privacy Policy, the following assessment was prepared:

| Requirement | Existing Policy Practice | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Transparency | Privacy Policy explains data collection and usage | Satisfactory |
| Consent | Explains collection and use of information | Further clarity needed |
| Purpose Limitation | Different purposes of data processing are described | Generally addressed |
| Data Security | Security procedures are mentioned | Implementation needs verification |
| Data Retention | Retention is based on reasonable requirements and applicable laws | More specific periods recommended |
| Data Deletion | Users can submit deletion requests | Retention exceptions need clarity |
| Location Privacy | Location collection and usage are explained | Greater transparency recommended |
| Third-Party Sharing | Categories of information recipients are identified | Further detail recommended |

**Overall Assessment:**

Porter has documented several important privacy practices in its published policy. However, a policy alone cannot prove complete regulatory compliance. Internal security controls, consent records, deletion procedures and third-party agreements would need to be examined for a complete assessment.
