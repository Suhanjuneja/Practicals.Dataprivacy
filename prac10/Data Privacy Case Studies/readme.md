# Practical 10: Data Privacy Case Studies

## 1. Aim

To study real-world data privacy incidents, understand their causes and impact, and identify measures to prevent similar privacy violations.

## 2. Objectives

- To understand major data privacy incidents.
- To identify causes of data breaches.
- To examine their impact on individuals and organizations.
- To understand the importance of data protection.
- To identify lessons for improving privacy practices.

## 3. Introduction

Data privacy case studies help us understand how personal information can be exposed, misused or inadequately protected. Studying real incidents highlights the importance of strong security, responsible data handling, privacy regulations and user awareness.

## 4. Case Study 1: Marriott International Data Breach (2018)

**Background:**

Marriott International disclosed a major data breach in 2018 involving its Starwood hotel reservation database. The unauthorized access had started in 2014 but remained undetected until 2018. Approximately 339 million guest records were affected.

**Privacy Issues:**
- Exposure of names, email addresses and phone numbers.
- Some passport and payment information was affected.
- Unauthorized access remained undetected for several years.
- Inadequate monitoring and protection of stored information.

**Impact:**

Millions of guest records were exposed, creating risks of identity theft and misuse of personal information.

**Lessons Learned:**
- Regularly monitor databases and networks.
- Protect sensitive information through encryption.
- Conduct security audits.
- Investigate suspicious activities promptly.
- Review security before acquiring another organization's systems.

## 5. Case Study 2: British Airways Data Breach (2018)

**Background:**

In 2018, British Airways experienced a cyberattack in which customer information entered on its website was collected through a fraudulent mechanism. Approximately 429,612 individuals were potentially affected.

**Privacy Issues:**
- Exposure of customer names and addresses.
- Payment card details were potentially accessed.
- Login credentials were also affected.
- Weaknesses in website security.

**Impact:**

Customers faced risks of financial fraud and misuse of their personal information. The incident also damaged trust in the company's online services.

**Lessons Learned:**
- Secure websites and online payment systems.
- Monitor unusual website activity.
- Protect customer login information.
- Conduct regular security testing.
- Inform affected individuals about incidents promptly.

## 6. Case Study 3: 23andMe Data Breach (2023)

**Background:**

23andMe is a genetic testing company that provides information about ancestry and genetic traits. In 2023, attackers accessed customer accounts using previously stolen login credentials. Through its DNA Relatives feature, information connected to millions of users was also accessed.

**Privacy Issues:**
- Exposure of genetic and ancestry-related information.
- Unauthorized access to customer accounts.
- Exposure of family relationship information.
- Inadequate protection against account-based attacks.

**Impact:**

Almost 7 million customers were affected. The incident raised serious concerns because genetic and family-related information is highly sensitive and difficult to change.

**Lessons Learned:**
- Encourage strong and unique passwords.
- Provide multi-factor authentication.
- Monitor suspicious login activity.
- Protect sensitive genetic information.
- Improve account security and incident response.

## 7. Case Study Comparison

| Case Study | Main Privacy Issue | Key Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Marriott (2018) | Exposure of hotel guest records | Database security and monitoring |
| British Airways (2018) | Exposure of customer and payment details | Website and payment security |
| 23andMe (2023) | Exposure of genetic and ancestry information | Strong account protection |

## 8. Common Privacy Lessons

1. **Data Security:** Protect personal information using appropriate security measures.
2. **Access Control:** Allow only authorized people to access sensitive data.
3. **Encryption:** Protect stored and transmitted information.
4. **Monitoring:** Identify suspicious activities quickly.
5. **User Awareness:** Encourage safe account practices.
6. **Incident Response:** Maintain a proper plan for handling data breaches.
7. **Accountability:** Organizations must take responsibility for protecting user information.

## 9. Recommendations

Organizations should:
- Conduct regular privacy and security audits.
- Use strong encryption and access controls.
- Implement multi-factor authentication.
- Regularly update software and systems.
- Monitor suspicious activities.
- Limit unnecessary data collection.
- Train employees in data protection.
- Maintain an effective breach response plan.

## 10. Conclusion

The three case studies demonstrate that data privacy failures can affect different types of personal information, including hotel records, payment details and genetic information. They highlight the importance of secure databases, website protection, strong authentication and continuous monitoring.

Organizations must adopt proper security measures, follow privacy regulations and handle personal information responsibly to prevent data breaches and maintain public trust.
